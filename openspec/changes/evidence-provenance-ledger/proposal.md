## Why

The report currently cannot distinguish model synthesis from facts about what the workflow actually executed: a failed consultation can appear successful, a model can name a consultation or source that never existed, and free-text supporting evidence has no enforceable link to content the report model actually received. Progress messages are presentation data rather than a trustworthy execution record, while the persisted strict outcome omits admission decisions, application-observed model steps, prompt assets, tool delivery, cache, dataset, and build facts needed to compare completed reports across changes.

## What Changes

- **BREAKING - strict report outcome v2**: replace string supporting evidence with structured items shaped as `{ statement, sourceRefs[] }`, add execution-quality metadata and strict `ProvenanceV1`, and update backend, frontend, REST, WebSocket, mock, downstream print/share/export, and tests as one coordinated wire migration.
- **Workflow-owned execution envelope**: `runDiagnosis` SHALL pass `formatReport` a versioned envelope containing the raw report-generation outcome and authoritative request batches, admitted consultations, linked generation/attempt/model-step records, tool records, retained source fragments, prompt usages, and runtime assets. The envelope SHALL be recorded directly from execution and SHALL NOT be reconstructed from progress.
- **Request/admission separation**: each CMO decision SHALL create a `RequestBatch` that records every requested entry with exactly one lowercase disposition (`admitted`, `invalid`, `duplicate`, or `budget_rejected`) plus a stable `reasonCode`. Invalid raw IDs are represented only by privacy-safe digests. Ordered-prefix admission is synchronous before scheduling; each admitted entry immediately creates a `ConsultationRecord` whose lifecycle is `admitted -> started -> succeeded|failed|cancelled`, with direct `admitted -> cancelled` allowed before worker start.
- **Observable generation hierarchy and budgets**: generation purpose SHALL be `cmo_decision`, `specialist_consultation`, or `final_report`; each `attemptId` SHALL identify exactly one application call to `Agent.generate` with phase `initial`, `retry`, or `correction`; and each `modelStepId` SHALL identify one application-observed Mastra sequential LLM step/provider call with a zero-based position and budget class `non_report`, `report_initial`, or `report_correction`. Explicit `maxSteps` and `prepareStep` guards SHALL cap observed model steps. Provenance SHALL NOT claim visibility into provider transport retries hidden inside an adapter.
- **Workflow-level tool provenance**: specialist-owned and CMO-owned tool records SHALL link to generation and attempt, plus model step where available, and separately capture terminal `executionStatus`, `transformStatus`, and `deliveryStatus`. Raw-result and safe-model-output digest nullability, privacy-safe public IDs, retrieval/cache facts, stable typed execution/transform/delivery codes, limitations, and tool/dataset versions SHALL be strict. Thrown execution or transform failure may deliver a canonical fail-safe envelope but remains failed on its own axis and is never eligible evidence.
- **Model-visible evidence catalog**: every canonical generic envelope SHALL identify one `sourceRecordId` and typed owner, and every enclosed fragment SHALL carry its exact `sourceFragmentId`, offsets, and encoded text. Evidence eligibility SHALL be built only from those exact bounded fragments retained after final context packing. Every succeeded consultation SHALL retain at least one bounded representation before report generation or the workflow SHALL complete with insufficient evidence instead of asking the model to summarize unseen content.
- **Bounded report state machine**: the `final_report` generation SHALL use one `initial` structured `Agent.generate` attempt and, only if needed, exactly one `correction` attempt. Both SHALL use `toolChoice: none` and `maxSteps: 1`; a bounded initial child deadline and internal settlement grace SHALL seal/detach uncooperative initial work before correction; CMO round decisions SHALL NOT carry an inline final report.
- **Whole-candidate validation**: each ranked diagnosis SHALL contain cited structured supporting evidence. Any unmatched consultation summary or structured consultation claim, or any missing, failed, undelivered, invisible, or unmatched source reference, SHALL invalidate the entire candidate. The initial candidate receives one correction; exhaustion SHALL return `generation_failed` with `REPORT_VALIDATION_FAILED`. Invalid content SHALL never be silently excluded, filtered, or sanitized into an available report.
- **Honest claim boundary**: source validation SHALL apply only to `supportingEvidence[].sourceRefs`. Rationales, consultation summaries, contradictory evidence, next steps, cross-specialty observations, and immediate actions remain model synthesis and SHALL NOT be presented as source-verified. Citation UI and exports SHALL state that references establish traceability, not clinical accuracy or truth.
- **Visible degraded execution**: failed consultations SHALL not be included in the succeeded consulted list, SHALL remain eligible for retry or a later-round reconsultation, and SHALL appear in privacy-safe degraded-execution metadata and an accessible warning. A failed consultation SHALL never be rendered with the green succeeded state, and a later success SHALL not erase the earlier failed record.
- **Shared prompt usage contract**: `PromptAssetUsageV1` SHALL record `promptId`, immutable manifest version/hash, separately resolved active-review `reviewId` and `reviewStatus`, structured actor, required attempt linkage, optional consultation linkage, and execution status equal to the linked attempt. Known manifest identity remains known when review lookup is unavailable; the all-`unknown` identity pair is reserved for unavailable manifest identity.
- **Versioned provenance**: strict `ProvenanceV1` SHALL include prompt schema metadata, linked generation/request-batch/attempt/model-step records, actual configured and provider-resolved model identities and request settings where observable, and explicit schema, app, lock, tool, and dataset versions. Unavailable values SHALL be `unknown`, never inferred defaults or silent omissions.
- **Completed-only durability**: only completed `available` or `generation_failed` outcomes SHALL persist provenance under the existing `JOB_TTL_MS` lifecycle. If the workflow itself fails, times out, or is cancelled, its in-memory recorder SHALL be discarded after status failure and SHALL NOT become a durable audit copy. Provenance-related logs SHALL contain only aggregate counts and stable codes. User-initiated downstream export is the only separate copy covered by this change.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `evidence-provenance-ledger`: Defines request/admission accounting, linked application-observed generation ledgers, model-visible source references, bounded whole-candidate reconciliation, degraded-execution disclosure, and completed-report provenance.

### Modified Capabilities

- `workflow-safety`: Adds a distinct requirement that non-admission, admitted consultation failure/cancellation, retry, reconsultation, and bounded model-step execution remain explicit without replacing the existing cancellation and timeout requirement.

## Impact

- **Backend workflow and hooks**: `src/backend/workflows/diagnostic-workflow.ts`, `src/backend/workflows/tool-event-hooks.ts`, and retry/context-packing paths gain request batches, the linked generation ledger, observed-step budgets, retained source fragments, whole-candidate validation, and the execution envelope.
- **Tools and cache**: existing tool adapters plus `src/backend/tools/utils/fetch.ts` and `tool-cache.ts` expose privacy-safe retrieval/cache metadata, public identifiers, raw and safe-output digests, delivery status, and typed limitations to the workflow recorder.
- **Shared and persistence contracts**: `src/shared/report-outcome.ts`, `src/backend/progress-store.ts`, and configuration/build metadata move together to strict v2. Completed outcomes retain provenance under `JOB_TTL_MS`; failed workflow status does not retain a provenance ledger.
- **Wire and frontend**: REST status, WebSocket completion, API parsing, job streaming, waiting status cards, results, citations, degraded warnings, and the shared evidence-source-label formatter consume only the new strict completed shape. Outcome-v2 backend/frontend and the `export-privacy-and-disclaimer` frontend integration deploy together after drain; legacy Print/Share cannot receive v2 evidence.
- **Mocks, tests, and documentation**: completed mock outcomes and failed-workflow paths plus backend, API, WebSocket, progress-store, frontend, and end-to-end fixtures cover the new contract; `AGENTS.md` documents observability limits, privacy, retention, deployment, and rollback.
- **Related change**: immutable manifest identity and active applicable review state from `clinician-reviewed-prompts` populate `PromptAssetUsageV1` separately. Known manifest version/hash remain known when review lookup is unavailable; the all-`unknown` identity pair is used only when manifest identity itself is unavailable.
